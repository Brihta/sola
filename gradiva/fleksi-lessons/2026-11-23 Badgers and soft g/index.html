🧩 Something new: soft g

A g can sound hard or soft.

🐐 goat · game · gold
🦒 giant · gem · gym

g often says /j/ before e, i or y.

But not always! get, girl, give, gift break the rule. 🤫

🤝 Practise together: All About Badgers

We finished the reading and checked our answers.

Badgers are nocturnal: they are active at night. They are omnivores: they eat plants and animals, and they love earthworms. 🪱

A badger's home is a sett: tunnels and chambers under the ground. A family of badgers is a clan, and a baby badger is a cub. Some setts are 100 years old!

Then we played hot seat 🪑: one of us was a badger and answered the class's questions using the new words.

🇸🇮 Badgers live in Slovenia too: a badger is a jazbec and its home is a jazbina.

✍️ On your own
