## ⏱ 7 minutes

📐 *Rosenshine: Present new material in small steps, provide models*

## 🧒 Pupils

1. Listen: is the **g** hard (like *goat*) or soft (like *giant*)?
2. Show **✊** for hard, **🖐** for soft.
3. Find the rule: what letter comes after a soft g?

## 🧑‍🏫 Teacher

- Say: game, gem, goat, giant, gym, gold, magic, gift
- Board: two columns, **hard g** and **soft g**. Sort with them.
- Rule: **g often says /j/ before e, i or y.**
- Challenge them: *get, girl, give, gift* break the rule. "The rule works most of the time, not always."
- Hand out the **Week 2 soft g** sheet. Read the 12 words together.
