## ⏱ 6 minutes

📐 *Rosenshine: Ask questions, obtain a high success rate*

## 🧒 Pupils

1. One pupil sits on the **hot seat**. They are a badger! 🦡
2. The class asks questions. For example: *Where do you live? What do you eat? Why do you sleep in the day? Who lives with you?*
3. The badger answers using these words: **sett, nocturnal, omnivore, clan, cub**

## 🧑‍🏫 Teacher

- 2 or 3 badgers, about 2 minutes each.
- Award a point for every text word used correctly.
- Link to home: badgers live in Slovenia too. **Badger = jazbec**, its home = **jazbina**. Has anyone seen one or its sett?
