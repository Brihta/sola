## ⏱ 6 minutes

## 🧒 Pupils

Write **4 or 5 sentences**: *A night in the life of a badger*.

You must use:
- **two** words from the text (sett, nocturnal, omnivore, clan, cub)
- **one** soft g word (giant, energy, imagine, magic …)

Finished? Draw a sett and label the tunnels and chambers.

## 🧑‍🏫 Teacher

- Short on time? Set this as homework.
- Ask 1 or 2 pupils to read their best sentence.
